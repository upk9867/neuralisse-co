1. Magnesium Glycinate

Magnesium is an essential mineral involved in normal nerve signaling, muscle function, and hundreds of biochemical reactions throughout the body. Individuals should check their total magnesium intake and consult a healthcare professional if they have kidney problems or take medications that may interact with magnesium.


2. Alpha-Lipoic Acid

Alpha-lipoic acid (ALA) is a naturally occurring compound involved in cellular energy metabolism and antioxidant processes. Researchers have investigated its potential role in managing symptoms associated with diabetic neuropathy. Some studies suggest possible benefits, but findings are mixed and do not establish a reliable treatment effect for everyone. Its presence in Neuralisse does not prove that the finished supplement relieves nerve discomfort or reverses nerve damage. People managing blood sugar levels should discuss its use with a healthcare professional.


3. Butcher’s Broom Root Extract

Butcher’s broom, scientifically known as Ruscus aculeatus, is a botanical ingredient traditionally used in products associated with vein and circulatory health. Its active plant compounds, including ruscogenins, have been studied for their potential effects on vascular function. However, research in this area does not establish that butcher’s broom improves nerve function or treats nerve-related conditions. Consumers should review the extract quantity and consult a healthcare professional before using it, particularly when taking medications.


4. L-Carnitine

L-carnitine is a naturally occurring compound that helps transport long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, where they can be used in cellular energy production. Researchers have also investigated certain forms of carnitine for possible effects on nerve health and discomfort. However, evidence varies depending on the form, dosage, and health condition being studied. Its inclusion in Neuralisse does not guarantee increased energy, improved nerve comfort, or relief from neuropathy. The actual amount per serving should be confirmed on the product label.


5. Turmeric Rhizome Extract

Turmeric is a botanical ingredient containing curcuminoids, including curcumin, which have been investigated for their antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. The supplied Neuralisse product information describes a turmeric extract standardized to 95% curcuminoids. This specification should be verified against the current Supplement Facts label. Although researchers continue to study curcumin for various health applications, available findings do not prove that turmeric extract in Neuralisse treats neuropathy, repairs damaged nerves, or provides specific clinical benefits.


6. Coenzyme Q10

Coenzyme Q10 (CoQ10), also known as ubiquinone, is a naturally occurring compound found in the body's cells. It plays an important role in mitochondrial energy production and also functions as an antioxidant. Researchers have explored CoQ10 for various aspects of cellular and cardiovascular health, but evidence for specific nerve-related benefits remains limited. The amount of CoQ10 in Neuralisse has not been provided in the available product information, so its quantity per serving cannot be assessed without checking the current product label.


7. Hypromellose

Hypromellose is a commonly used ingredient in dietary supplement manufacturing, particularly for vegetarian and plant-based capsule shells. It helps hold the supplement ingredients together in a convenient capsule format and provides an alternative to certain animal-derived capsule materials. Hypromellose is generally used as a formulation ingredient rather than an active nutritional component. Its presence in Neuralisse does not indicate a direct effect on nerve health or neurological function.


8. Microcrystalline Cellulose

Microcrystalline cellulose is a widely used excipient in dietary supplements and pharmaceutical products. It commonly functions as a filler, binder, or manufacturing aid, helping provide appropriate bulk, texture, and consistency to capsules and tablets. It is not considered an active ingredient for nerve health and is primarily included for formulation purposes. Consumers with specific ingredient sensitivities should review the complete Neuralisse label to understand all inactive ingredients before use.
